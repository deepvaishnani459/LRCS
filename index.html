Give an overview of the main points.

30
CASE STUDY

Tip 1 · In practice

Watch the synthesis

EXAMPLE PARAGRAPH — OUR CASE Perloff (2014) theorizes that the interactive aspects of social media may influence its impact on body image, noting young women are among the most active users. Several empirical studies focus on Facebook usage in adolescent girls (Tiggermann & Slater, 2013; Meier & Gray, 2014) and young adult women (Smith, Hames & Joiner, 2013; Fardouly et al., 2015; Cohen, Newton-John & Slater, 2017), while a systematic review by Holland & Timmerman (2016) confirmed the link for both women and men.

1 THEORY + 6 STUDIES → one synthesized trend.

CASE STUDY

Tip 2 · Analyze & Interpret

Add your interpretation

Discuss the significance of findings in relation to the literature as a whole.

OUR CONCLUSION →Visual & interactive aspects are the most important factor in body image issues.
“Body image issues are influenced not by social media usage in general, but by engagement with the visual and interactive aspects of these platforms.”

Tip 3 · Critically Evaluate

Strengths & weaknesses

Contribute your own judgement of the sources.

“In an era of rapidly changing digital technologies, the mass media paradigm is no longer adequate… findings of older studies may not be relevant to younger generations.”
IN OUR CASE →Older mass-media findings ≠ today’s Gen Z platforms.
